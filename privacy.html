NexusLink Privacy Policy

Google Play Store & GDPR Compliant • Last Updated: October 24, 2026

NexusLink ("we", "our", or "the Platform") provides an open, decentralized community seeding and anti-fraud affiliate network. We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy and upholding transparent data practices. This policy outlines how information is handled when using our website and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver automated affiliate escrow settlements and combat automated bot abuse, we collect minimal necessary data:

  • Account Details: Email address, pseudonym/organization name, and encrypted credentials.
  • Wallet & Payment Data: Public cryptographic wallet address or UPI/INR billing identifiers for issuing authorized payouts.
  • Fraud Detection Telemetry: Anonymized HTTP headers, client IP addresses (evaluated for datacenter ASNs and VPN/Tor subnets), and user-agent strings strictly for malicious bot suppression.

2. How We Use Collected Information

  • To authenticate and manage promoter and advertiser account vaults.
  • To enforce smart-escrow payouts (60% promoter reward / 40% platform treasury split).
  • To intercept and quarantine automated click-fraud, scrapers, and headless browsers.
  • To generate double-entry financial journal logs and provide auditability.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell or monetize your personal information. Data is only processed through secure infrastructure providers solely required to keep the service operational and combat fraudulent abuse.

4. Data Retention & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/HTTPS), salted password hashing, and strict database access controls. Audit trail logs are preserved to satisfy financial accountability regulations.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

Under Google Play Store requirements and international data protection laws (GDPR/CCPA), users maintain the unconditional right to access, export, or permanently erase their personal account data at any time.

To delete your account and associated personal identifiers, visit our Account Deletion Portal or email us directly at privacy@nexuslink.network.
